About Me

In 2020, I enrolled in a graphic design program to make my life-long dream of becoming an artist a reality. However, I unfortunately realized I found the work unfulfilling and dull during my last semester. Though I never found enjoyment in graphic design, pursuing that dream opened a new path for me when I took a web development class.Using logic to style and build a website was satisfying in a way that graphic design had never been, and in 2023 I decided to completely switch gears by going back to school for computer programming. It was very difficult at times, but I had never been so motivated to learn and overcome challenges before.

I am now a full-time software engineer with one year of experience under my belt. In that year I have gained extensive experience in using SQL Server, C#, Snaplogic, and Cloudera to build backend software that retrieves, transforms, and migrates data. In addition, I have used AWS services such as Lambda, Glue, Athena, and S3 to add additional features to an existing data pipeline. I am also well acquainted with using APIs and SQL to gather specific information, and use these skills daily to quickly analyize production data issues at my current job and implement a solution. Through my time in school I also have experience in Java, Javascript, Python, and web development.
